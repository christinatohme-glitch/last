Talyfocous - Microsoft Store Description
=======================================

Short Description
Local-first accounting software for invoices, inventory, payroll, bank reconciliation, and reports. Data stays on your PC.

Description
Talyfocous is a Windows accounting system for small businesses and accountants who want a fast, local-first desktop app.

It helps you manage:
- Journal vouchers for invoices, receipts, payments, expenses, and adjustments
- Chart of accounts, account statements, and currency tracking
- Financial reports including trial balance, balance sheet, profit and loss, and cash flow
- Inventory workflows for products, stock movements, purchase invoices, and sales invoices
- Payroll templates and tax-related pages
- Bank reconciliation and common file imports such as PDF, Excel, Word, CSV, and Google Sheets

Key Benefits
- Works offline because your data is stored locally on the device
- Supports multiple companies and shared workflows when local network sharing is enabled
- Opens from a desktop shortcut after Store installation
- Keeps accounting, reporting, and inventory in one system
